## Design Context

### Users
ChatGPT Helper is for heavy ChatGPT users who keep long conversations, reusable prompts, exports, and navigation aids close at hand. They are usually working, studying, researching, writing, or coding, and need the extension to feel fast, predictable, and non-intrusive beside the native ChatGPT page.

### Brand Personality
Quiet, precise, capable. The interface should feel like a focused productivity companion: calm enough for long sessions, polished enough to inspire confidence, and restrained enough to stay out of the user's main conversation flow.

### Aesthetic Direction
Use a quiet efficiency direction. The sidebar should be visually close to ChatGPT's native surfaces, with softened contrast, subtle depth, clearer active states, and consistent spacing. Avoid loud gradients, heavy shadows, decorative glass effects by default, and overly playful tool styling.

### Design Principles
- Keep the conversation primary; the helper should support the workflow without competing for attention.
- Use spacing, borders, and subtle surface shifts to create hierarchy before adding color.
- Preserve density for power users while making scan paths clear and touch targets reliable.
- Make hover, focus, active, selected, and disabled states visible but calm.
- Keep the implementation compatible with existing extension settings, themes, and content-script behavior.
